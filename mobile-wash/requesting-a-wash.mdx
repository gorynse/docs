Request an on-site wash from **Mobile Wash → Work Orders** in [app.gorynse.com](https://app.gorynse.com). Rynse assigns the right vendor for your facility — you don't pick one.

## Submit a request

Click **Request work order**, then work through three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Location & vehicles">
    Pick the **facility** the crew should come to. Its address shows underneath. Need the crew somewhere else, like a staging area or second gate? Click **Edit** and enter that address. If the facility has no address saved, add one here before continuing.

    Then choose how to request:

    - **Pick Specific or Add New Vehicles** — tick the vehicles you want, or click **Select all**. Search by make, model, plate, or asset ID. You can enter several terms at once, or paste a column of plates or asset IDs from a spreadsheet. Any term with no match is highlighted so you can catch a typo.
    - **Estimate vehicle count** — enter a number. Specific vehicles get assigned later.

    <Note>
      The list shows your whole active fleet, with the selected facility's vehicles first, so you can include a vehicle assigned to another location. Archived vehicles don't appear.
    </Note>

    <Tip>
      **Vehicle not in your fleet yet?** Click **Add a New Vehicle**, enter its VIN, license plate, or asset ID, then click **Add vehicle**. It's saved to your account under the selected facility and included in this request.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Services">
    Choose what's needed: **Exterior**, **Interior**, **Detail**, **Trailer**, **Biohazard**, or **Custom**. Nothing is pre-selected.

    - **Specific vehicles:** check services under **Services to apply**, tick the vehicles in the table, then click **Apply to selected** — or **Apply to all**. Mix and match, for example Exterior + Interior on a tractor and Trailer on its trailer. Every vehicle needs at least one service. If you change your picks after applying, apply again or click **Discard changes** before moving on.
    - **Estimated count:** the services you pick apply to every vehicle.

    Biohazard and Custom need a note on the next step describing what you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule & notes">
    - **Earliest** and **Latest** — the vendor comes on one day in this window. The earliest date is at least two days from today. A wider window is easier to fit.
    - **Arrive after** / **Arrive before** (optional) — a preferred time of day. It's a preference, not a guarantee.
    - **Purchase Order** — enter a PO number, or tick **Not Required**.
    - **Notes** — gate codes, on-site contact, where to stage vehicles.

    Check **Review your request**, then click **Submit request**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your request appears on **Work Orders** as **Requested**. You can delete it there while it's still Requested.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can one work order cover multiple facilities?">
    No — one facility per work order. Submit a separate request for each location.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request a wash for tomorrow?">
    No. The earliest date is two days from today, so the vendor has time to schedule a crew.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request a specific arrival time?">
    You can request an arrival window with **Arrive after** and **Arrive before**. It's a preference, not a guarantee. Once the wash is scheduled, the work order shows an **Estimated arrival**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use a vendor I already work with?">
    Rynse assigns the vendor for you. For a new vendor relationship, contact your account team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reuse a one-off address?">
    Yes. Click **Saved** on the address to pick one you've entered before.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will I see pricing?">
    Not on the request form. Pricing shows on the work order once it's completed, itemized per vehicle and service, and on your invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set up a recurring wash?">
    Not from the request form. Recurring visits are arranged with your account team and show up on the **Schedule** view.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
