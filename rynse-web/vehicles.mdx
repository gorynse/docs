The **Organization → Vehicles** page at [app.gorynse.com](https://app.gorynse.com) is the source of truth for which vehicles are enrolled in your program. Use it to confirm what's in the fleet, spot vehicles missing a card, and check what each vehicle is approved to spend on.

<Info>
  **Most changes go through Rynse** — archiving a vehicle, swapping a card between vehicles, ordering new cards, and changing memberships. See [Asking us to make changes](#asking-us-to-make-changes). A few things you can do yourself: edit a vehicle's **External ID** (click its row), [move vehicles to another facility](#changing-a-vehicles-facility), [freeze or close cards](#actions-you-can-take-from-this-page), and add a vehicle while [requesting a mobile wash](/mobile-wash/requesting-a-wash).
</Info>

## How it's organized

The page opens on a table of every active vehicle, one row each. Click a row for full detail. Four tabs at the top right switch what's listed:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every active vehicle |
| **No Card** | Active vehicles with no Rynse card attached |
| **Card** | Active vehicles that have a card |
| **Archived** | Vehicles decommissioned from the program |

<Tip>
  **No Card** is a quick audit. If you expect every vehicle to have one, anything here is either waiting for activation or was missed during onboarding. Email support and we'll dig in.
</Tip>

### Membership filter

If any vehicles are enrolled in wash memberships, a **Membership** filter appears next to the tabs. Use it to show only vehicles on one vendor and plan (for example, "Quick Quack - Unlimited"). Pick **All** to clear it.

## What the columns mean

Most columns are self-explanatory — **Created**, **Facility**, **Description**, **VIN**, **License Plate**, and **Asset Type**. A few are worth calling out:

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **External ID** | Your own asset number — shows on tables and invoices so you can reconcile to your records |
| **RFID** | The vehicle's RFID tag, if your wash program uses RFID readers. Contact support to change it |
| **Card** | Last 4 of the attached card. A red card icon means no card; a bank icon means an on-demand funding account |
| **Name on Card** | The name embossed on the card. Many fleets use it as a department or account code |
| **Spend Policy** | What this card can be used for — hover for details |
| **Services** | Wash programs the vehicle is enrolled in — hover for the vendor list |

<Note>
  A **Memberships** column is hidden by default to keep the table readable. Click the columns icon above the header to turn it on. The **Services** column shows the same thing at a glance.
</Note>

### Spend Policy

Hover the Spend Policy column for the rules on a card. A **padlock** means spend-category restrictions are configured (not that the card is frozen); a **clock** means an active schedule. A gray label instead of a policy name means an organization-wide rule applies because the vehicle has no policy of its own. Hovering shows up to four things:

- **Policy** — the named spend policy and its rules (per-transaction cap, monthly cap, allowed merchants)
- **Organization Rules** — rules that apply across your whole organization
- **Merchant Rules** — approved spend categories (Car Wash, Fuel Gas, EV Charging, Preventative Maintenance)
- **Schedule** — any time-of-day or day-of-week restrictions

See [Spend Controls](/rynse-web/spend-controls) for what each rule does.

### Services

Icons show the wash programs a vehicle is enrolled in: **pay-as-you-go** (pay per wash), **unlimited** (flat monthly fee, unlimited washes at that vendor — charged only in months the vehicle actually washes), and **mobile wash** (on-site washes brought to your facility — see [Mobile Wash](/mobile-wash/overview)). Hover an icon for the vendors covered; a dash (`-`) means no wash program. A **snowflake** marks a frozen card.

### Duplicate license plate warnings

Some wash vendors recognize vehicles by license plate. If the same plate is enrolled more than once on one of those plans, a red banner above the table names the plan and the duplicated plates, and flags any that belong to an **archived** vehicle. Duplicates can send a vehicle's washes to the wrong record, so email `support@gorynse.com` with the plates shown and we'll clean them up.

## Asking us to make changes

Changes to the roster — adding a vehicle (unless you're adding it while [requesting a mobile wash](/mobile-wash/requesting-a-wash)), editing details, swapping a card to another vehicle, archiving, or attaching or canceling a wash membership — all go through Rynse. We coordinate the vendor side so you don't have to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email support@gorynse.com">
    Send an email to `support@gorynse.com` describing what you need. If your organization uses the Rynse vehicle request form (a Smartsheet link shared during onboarding), you can submit there instead — either way you'll get a confirmation receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the vehicle details">
    For each new vehicle, send: **Description** (year, make, model, and your internal name), **VIN**, **License plate** and state, **External ID**, and the **Facility** to assign it to.

    For changes to existing vehicles, just include enough to identify it — VIN or External ID works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We handle the vendor side">
    Your account team coordinates with the wash vendors to enroll, re-enroll, or remove the vehicle. The list updates once the change is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Decommissioning a vehicle?** Ask us to _archive_ it rather than delete it. Archiving stops billing and removes it from dashboard filters, but keeps the transaction history under the **Archived** tab.
</Tip>

### Fleetio customers

<Note>
  See a blue **Synced with Fleetio** banner above the table? Fleetio is your system of record — make vehicle changes in Fleetio, not by emailing support, and they flow into Rynse automatically. That includes facilities: each vehicle's facility follows its Fleetio **Group**, so **Change Facility** is turned off here. A vehicle's **External ID** is replaced with its Fleetio vehicle name at each sync, so rename vehicles in Fleetio. Freezing and closing cards still happen in Rynse. See the [Fleetio integration](/integrations/fleetio) for what syncing covers.
</Note>

## What to expect after a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rynse confirms receipt">
    You'll get a confirmation email (or a form receipt). If nothing arrives within a few hours, email `support@gorynse.com` to confirm before assuming it's in progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rynse coordinates with the vendor">
    Your account team works the vendor side — enrolling or removing the vehicle, updating license plate recognition (LPR) records, and issuing or closing the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change goes live">
    - **New vehicles:** Typically active within **3–5 business days**, depending on vendor processing.
    - **Deactivations and removals:** Allow **24 to 48 hours** for wash access to fully close. During that window the vehicle may still complete a wash, and you may be responsible for charges incurred before deactivation finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **If the timeline has passed and the vehicle still isn't active**, don't assume it's still in progress — activation can stall if a vendor hasn't processed the enrollment. Email `support@gorynse.com` with the vehicle's VIN or plate and reference your original request, and we'll check the vendor-side status and push it through.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  For urgent activations, say so when you email support and we can escalate to the vendor.
</Tip>

## Vehicle history follows the vehicle, not the card

Every wash, fuel stop, and visit is attached to the **vehicle**, not the card that paid. When a card is closed and reissued, the new card inherits the full history — so closing a card never loses it.

## Actions you can take from this page

Tick one or more rows and use the buttons below the table (hover a button to see its name). You can also freeze, unfreeze, or close a single card from a vehicle's detail view.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Freeze Card" icon="snowflake">
    Pause the selected cards. Transactions are blocked until you unfreeze them with the same button. Use when a vehicle is out of service or a card is misplaced.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Close Card" icon="ban">
    Permanently close the selected cards, or [a whole list from a CSV](#closing-cards-in-bulk-from-a-csv). Use for confirmed lost, stolen, or compromised cards.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Change Facility" icon="building">
    Move vehicles to a different facility. See [Changing a vehicle's facility](#changing-a-vehicles-facility).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  **Close Card is permanent.** A closed card can't be reopened, and the vehicle needs a new card. If you're not sure a card is gone for good, freeze it instead and email support.
</Warning>

### Closing cards in bulk from a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Close Card">
    No need to select rows first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your CSV">
    Under **Close from CSV**, upload a list of VINs, license plates, or External IDs — one per cell, or under a `VIN`, `License Plate`, or `External ID` header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Rynse shows each matching vehicle with an active card, plus anything it couldn't find or that has no card. Check the list, then confirm to close them all.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing a vehicle's facility

You can move vehicles to a different facility yourself. The new facility shows in the portal and on your invoices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the vehicles">
    On **Organization → Vehicles**, tick the checkbox on each row you want to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Facility">
    Below the table. Pick the new facility and click **Submit Change Facility**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To move a single vehicle, you can also click its row, pick a new **Facility**, and click **Save Changes**.

### Changing facilities in bulk from a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Change Facility">
    No need to select rows first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where vehicles go">
    Pick one facility for everyone, or add a **Facility** column to your CSV with the facility name on each row. Rows without a facility use the one you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your CSV">
    Under **Change facility from CSV**, upload a list of VINs, license plates, or External IDs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Rynse shows each vehicle's current and new facility. Rows it can't use (no match, unknown facility, or already there) are listed by line number so you can fix the file and re-upload. Click **Move** to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Changing a facility only changes the facility.** It doesn't enroll the vehicle in a wash membership, change an existing membership, or request a Rynse card. For those, email `support@gorynse.com`.
</Note>

## A note on timing

Transactions and visits reflect what vendors have reported to us. Vendor usage reports typically arrive in the first week of each month for the previous month — so a brand-new vehicle won't show usage right away, even if drivers are washing.

## Common card questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do drivers need to activate the cards?">
    No — cards arrive ready to use. The activation slip in the envelope is for a different program; throw it away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do drivers need to sign the back of the card?">
    No. The card is tied to a vehicle, not a person.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The last 4 doesn't match my asset number — how do I match a card to a vehicle?">
    Card numbers are random, not sequential by vehicle. Use the **External ID** column or the **Name on Card** field — many fleets put the unit number or department there.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get driver-level attribution?">
    No — every transaction is attributed to the vehicle, not the driver. To control timing or usage, ask about schedule and per-transaction caps in [Spend Controls](/rynse-web/spend-controls).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I order more cards from the portal?">
    No — email `support@gorynse.com` with the vehicles that need cards and we'll handle production and shipping.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The name on the card is wrong — can it be changed?">
    No. The name is physically embossed and can't be changed after production. If you need a different label, request a re-issue.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
